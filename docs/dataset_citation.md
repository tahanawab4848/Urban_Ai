# Dataset Citation & Provenance Guide

**Project:** Urban Air Quality Category Prediction (Multi-Class Classification)  
**Curriculum:** Learn Depth Academy — Track 1 Final Capstone (Problem 10)  

---

## Dataset Overview
- **Dataset Title:** Air Quality Data in India (2015–2020)
- **Primary Data File:** `city_day.csv`
- **Dataset Curator:** Rohan Rao
- **Hosting Platform:** Kaggle
- **Dataset URL:** [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india)
- **Publication Year:** 2020
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0: Public Domain
- **Original Data Origin:** Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India ([https://cpcb.nic.in](https://cpcb.nic.in))
- **Temporal Coverage:** January 1, 2015 – July 1, 2020
- **Spatial Coverage:** 26 major metropolitan and industrial urban centers in India

## 5. Regulatory Standard Citation

When referencing the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) National Air Quality Index (NAQI) category breakpoints (*Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe*), reference the official technical steering document:

```text
Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). (2014). National Air Quality Index Report. Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India, New Delhi.
```
